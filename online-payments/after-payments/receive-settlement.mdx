---
title: Receiving Settlements
---

All businesses on SeerBit are paid automatically, this means as a SeerBit Merchant you do not have to request for your business settlement. After deducting our charges, we pay the entire sum of transactions received in a day into your bank account. However, settlements availability depends on some factors such as the business country and industry your business is operating in.

> **When does a merchant receive their settlement?**

Naira transactions are on a settlements schedule of T+1 - this means that we automatically pay the total sum received to the merchant on the next working day after the transaction day.

> **International settlement in Nigeria?**

To get your settlements for USD transactions you will need a [United Bank for Africa Domiciliary account](https://www.ubagroup.com/nigeria/personal-banking/accounts/domiciliary-account/).
