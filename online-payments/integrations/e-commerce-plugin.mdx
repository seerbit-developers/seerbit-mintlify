**Ideal for**: Taking payments on a store you did not build the checkout for — WooCommerce, Magento, Wix, Zoho Books or Ecwid. You install a plugin and paste in your keys; there is no code to write.

SeerBit's official plugins are secure and PCI-compliant, and support the same [payment methods](/payment-methods/overview) as every other integration.

## Plugin or your own integration?

| | E-commerce plugin | [Simple](/online-payments/integrations/simple-checkout) or [Standard Checkout](/online-payments/integrations/standard-checkout) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What you write** | Nothing — configuration only | Code, on your page or your server |
| **Where it runs** | Inside your store platform | Your own site and server |
| **Control of the flow** | Whatever the plugin exposes | Complete |
| **Orders and inventory** | The platform already handles them | You handle them |
| **Time to first payment** | Minutes | Hours to days |
| **Best for** | A store on a supported platform | A custom storefront, an unsupported platform, or a flow the plugin cannot express |

**If your store runs on one of the platforms below, use the plugin.** Build a direct integration only when you have your own storefront, or need something the plugin does not offer — a bespoke checkout, a split rule per order, or a payment step inside a non-commerce flow.

## How it works

The plugin adds SeerBit as a payment option at your store's existing checkout. When a customer pays, the plugin calls SeerBit, takes them through the payment, and reports the result back to the platform so the order is marked paid. Transactions appear in your SeerBit dashboard alongside every other payment.

## Before you start

- **Retrieve your API keys**: log in to your [SeerBit dashboard](https://dashboard.seerbit.com/#/auth/login) and go to **Settings > API Keys**.
- **Start in test mode**: configure the plugin with your **test keys** and complete at least one successful [test transaction](/test-and-live) before switching to live.

## After installation

- **Test every payment method you plan to offer**, using the [test cards](/test-and-live) — not just cards.
- **Check the order is marked paid** in your platform, not only that the payment succeeded in SeerBit. A payment that does not update the order is the most common plugin problem.
- **Confirm the result server-side** where your platform allows it — see [Verify a payment](/online-payments/after-payments/verify-payment).
- **Switch to live keys** and run one small real transaction before you open the store. See [Go live](/go-live).

## Notes

- **Plugins are configured, not coded.** If you need behaviour the plugin does not expose — custom split rules, a bespoke payment step — build a [direct integration](/online-payments/integrations/accept-payment) instead.
- **Test and live keys are separate.** A plugin configured with test keys takes no real money; swapping the keys is what makes the store live.
- **Plugin updates come from the platform**, not from SeerBit. Keep the plugin current through your platform's own update mechanism.
- Questions about a specific plugin? Reach [SeerBit Support](https://seerbitteam.freshdesk.com/support/home).
