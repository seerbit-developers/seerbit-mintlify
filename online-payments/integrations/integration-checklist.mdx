Welcome! This checklist is your step-by-step guide to building a complete, robust online payment system with SeerBit. Follow these phases in order, from your first test to going live.

> Follow the checklist below to ensure a complete implementation into SeerBit

## Phase 1: Set Up & Start Testing

### Create Your Account & Validate Email

- **Sign Up**: [Register](https://dashboard.seerbit.com/#/auth/register) for a free SeerBit account to access your dashboard.

![image of seerbit sign up page](/assets/register.png)

- **Verify Email**: Check your inbox for a verification link and click it to confirm your email address.

![image of seerbit verification email](/assets/email-template.jpg)

- **Login**: Once verified, log in using the credentials you created.

![image of seerbit login page](/assets/login.png)

### Retrieve Your API Keys

-**Test Mode**: By default, your account starts in _Test Mode_, allowing you to integrate using test API keys without submitting business credentials.

![image of seerbit dashboard home page](/assets/dashboard.png)

- **Access Keys**: Navigate to **Settings \> API Keys** in your dashboard to copy your test keys.

![image of seerbit dashboard settings page](/assets/keys-page.png)

- **Live Keys**: Available only after your business is approved.

![image of seerbit dashboard settings page](/assets/live-key.png)

### Make Your First Test Payment

- **Choose Your Integration Method**: Pick the method that fits your needs:

  - **Simple Checkout**: An inline, PCI-compliant payment form that works seamlessly across devices. [Learn more](/online-payments/integrations/simple-checkout).
  - **Standard Checkout**: Generate a secure checkout link via the /payment/initialize endpoint. [Learn more](/online-payments/integrations/standard-checkout).
  - **E-commerce Plugins**: Integrate SeerBit with popular platforms. [Learn more](/online-payments/integrations/e-commerce-plugin).
- **Run a Test Transaction**: Use our [test card](/testing/overview) to simulate a successful payment. No real funds are debited.

## Phase 2: Implement Real-Time Notifications

Enable instant updates for your transactions.

- Set Up Webhooks: Configure a webhook URL in your dashboard to receive POST requests whenever an event occurs (e.g., transaction status updates). Each notification includes an eventType indicating the trigger. [Learn more](/online-payments/after-payments/webhook-events).

## Phase 3: Prepare to Go Live

Switch from testing to processing real payments.

### Submit Business for Approval

- **Complete Onboarding**: Upload all required KYC documents in your [dashboard](https://dashboard.seerbit.com/#/auth/login).
- **Wait for Approval**: Our team will review your application and notify you once your live account is activated.

![image of seerbit onboarding page](/assets/onboarding.png)

### Execute the Go-Live Switch

- **Swap API Keys**: Replace test keys with your Live Keys in your code.

![image of seerbit settings page](/assets/go-live.png)

- **Update Webhook Endpoint**: Ensure your production webhook URL is configured in the live dashboard.

![image of seerbit settings page](/assets/webhook.png)

- **Perform a Final Live Test**: Process a small real transaction to confirm the entire flow works in the live environment.

## Make a Payment

The core of your SeerBit integration is being able to make payments and handle their subsequent states.

1. **Make a payment**<br /> Make a successful payment with any of our [Online Payment](/online-payments/integrations/accept-payment) Integrations.
2. **Refund a payment**<br /> It is important to handle situations where you may need to cancel or refund a payment.
3. **Integrate additional payment methods**<br /> ‍Add additional [Payment Methods](/payment-methods/overview) to your account for your shoppers countries. It is also important you can handle the different [Status Codes](/development-resources/responses/seerbit-status-code), such as pending, which is more common for local payment methods.

## Webhooks

SeerBit uses [webhooks](/online-payments/after-payments/webhook-events) to inform your system on payment statuses, results of actions such as refunds, receiving the payment result of some local payment methods, and other events.

1. **Expose an endpoint to handle webhooks events**<br /> You need to expose an endpoint in your system to be able to receive our webhooks events.
2. **Configure the webhooks to send to your endpoint**<br /> Login to your merchant dashboard in order to set the endpoint of your webhook handler.
3. **Receive and accept a webhook event**<br /> Successfully handle and process our different types of webhook event code.

## Going Live

To accept payments from your customers, your business needs to have been approved, complete your onboarding process and KYC document upload.<br /> If your application for a live account is approved, follow our go-live checklist to start accepting live payments.
