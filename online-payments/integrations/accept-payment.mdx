Build a payment checkout or use SeerBit's prebuilt checkout page to accept online payments

## SeerBit Checkout

The SeerBit Checkout is a hosted payment page that allows you to collect payments securely and fast using any of our [payment methods](/payment-methods/overview). The checkout works on all devices and help in increasing conversion rate for your customers making payments on your website or application

- Mobile Responsive - Fully responsive design
- International - Supports multiple [currencies](/development-resources/currency-codes/overview) and [payment methods](/payment-methods/overview)
- Checkout Adverts - Display adverts of your products or services to customers on checkout
- Compliance - Fully PCI compliant checkout

## Integration Types

Accept payments on your website or mobile applications with seerBit using our multiple payment methods.

- [Simple Checkout](/online-payments/integrations/simple-checkout) - Simple Checkout our simplest integration method and requires little technical knowledge. SeerBit inline code is a smart payment form that works seamlessly across devices and is designed to increase your conversion rate. It supports all the payment methods you get with the checkout standard integration.
- [Standard Checkout](/online-payments/integrations/standard-checkout) - SeerBit Checkout Standard Initialises a transaction from merchants server to generate a payment link which redirects your customers to a page to make payment and after payment has been made the customer is redirected back to the merchants website.
- [SDKs & Libraries](/sdks/overview) - Our Software Development Kits remove the need to handle sensitive data by enabling you to process payments and transactions in the same secure way as Simple Checkout and Standard Checkout.
- [E-commerce Plugins](/online-payments/integrations/e-commerce-plugin) - If your store already runs on WooCommerce, Magento, Wix, Zoho Books or Ecwid, install the plugin and paste in your keys. No code. See [plugin or your own integration?](/online-payments/integrations/e-commerce-plugin#plugin-or-your-own-integration) to decide.

> <b>To make API calls on SeerBit, you'll have to pass a Bearer Token. To generate a bearer token you simply</b>
>
> - Call [https://seerbitapi.com/api/v2/encrypt/keys](https://seerbitapi.com/api/v2/encrypt/keys)
> - Pass your secret and public key.
> - You'll be authenticated and receive a token to process further API calls.
