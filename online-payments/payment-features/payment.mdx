## After Payments

After the payment, several things can happen, if the payment succeeds normally, you can focus on payout options from SeerBit and triggering workflows to fulfill the customer’s order. When it’s more complicated, SeerBit provides guidance for things like declined cards, refunds, disputes and fraud.
