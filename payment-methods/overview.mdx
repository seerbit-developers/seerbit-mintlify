SeerBit supports several ways for your customers to pay. Every method is available through both [Simple Checkout](/online-payments/integrations/simple-checkout) and [Standard Checkout](/online-payments/integrations/standard-checkout), and all of them initialise through the same endpoint — `POST /api/v2/payments/initiates` — with a different `paymentType`.

Offering more than one method generally raises conversion, because customers differ in what they have available.

## Choosing a method

| If you want to | Use |
| -------------- | --- |
| Accept the widest range of customers online | [Card payments](/payment-methods/card-payment) |
| Avoid card scheme fees on larger amounts | [Transfer](/payment-methods/transfer) or [bank account](/payment-methods/bank-account) |
| Reach customers without smartphones or cards | [USSD](/payment-methods/ussd-payment) |
| Collect in markets where wallets dominate | [Mobile money](/payment-methods/mobile-money) |
| Charge a customer standing in front of you | [In-store POS](/in-store/overview) |

## Building your own checkout

The pages above document the direct API — `POST /api/v2/payments/initiates` — for merchants who want to control how the payment interface looks. Two things follow from that:

- **Card payments require PCI DSS certification.** Your server would be receiving raw card details, so SeerBit will not enable [card payments](/payment-methods/card-payment) on your account without a valid certificate. Every other method is unaffected.
- **You calculate the transaction fee.** Unlike the hosted checkout, these endpoints charge exactly the amount you send — so if the customer bears the fee, you must add it. See [transaction fees](/online-payments/integrations/standard-checkout#transaction-fees).

Neither applies if you use [Simple Checkout](/online-payments/integrations/simple-checkout), [Standard Checkout](/online-payments/integrations/standard-checkout) or a [plugin](/online-payments/integrations/e-commerce-plugin).

## Before you go live

Some methods settle asynchronously — a customer can leave the page before the payment resolves. Always confirm the outcome with [payment verification](/online-payments/after-payments/verify-payment) or [webhooks](/online-payments/after-payments/webhook-events) rather than the browser redirect, and handle the `PENDING` state described in the [status codes](/development-resources/responses/seerbit-status-code) reference.

Test credentials for every method are on the [testing page](/test-and-live).
